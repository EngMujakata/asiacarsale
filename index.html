Export Operations & Purchase Protocol

AsiaCarSale coordinates direct vehicle acquisitions from verified suppliers and export hubs in Thailand, managing complete freight arrangements designated for port delivery to regional importers.

  1. Unit Selection & Verification: Review specific engineering configurations, engine capacity, mileage, and documentation status.
  2. Secure Wire Transfer: Complete institutional payment through designated banking channels and submit transaction verification documents.
  3. Export Processing & Shipping: Vehicles undergo export inspection, de-registration, and booking on ro-ro or container vessels.
  4. Documentation Handover: Original Bills of Lading and export certificates are dispatched via secure courier.
  5. Port Clearance Logistics: Importers manage regional port customs clearance and final transport coordination.
VEHICLE SELECTION → WIRE SETTLEMENT → EXPORT PROCESSING → VESSEL TRANSIT → DOCUMENT DISPATCH → CUSTOMS CLEARANCE

Regulatory Notice & Liability

Operational scope covers vehicle acquisition within Thailand and scheduled maritime freight delivery up to the agreed discharge port (Beira, Durban, or alternative regional gateways).

Clients maintain full statutory responsibility for local import duties, surcharges, regulatory compliance testing, and inland carrier transportation from the destination discharge terminal.
